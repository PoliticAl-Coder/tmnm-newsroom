Owner-operated publishing application

TMNM Owner Console

A private editorial workflow application for Truth Matters News Media. It is designed to help its authorised operator manage source documents, review articles and plan publishing activity.

Current status: pre-production. This informational site does not sign anyone into Google, collect credentials, offer public accounts, or activate scheduling or publishing. Google account connections and publication functions remain subject to separate owner approval and technical verification.

What the application is for

Editorial documents

Prepare and review operator-selected material used in TMNM editorial workflows, including eligible files on Google Drive and Google Docs when specifically authorised.

Google integration

The existing desktop integration has code paths for Google Drive, Docs and Apps Script operations. Requested permissions are subject to the connected Google account's consent and verified actual configuration.

Controlled publishing

Support editorial checks and future owner-approved publishing workflows. This page does not itself post to platforms or certify that current platform connections are working.

Transparency and Google data

The Google OAuth configuration, including requested access, data handling, storage, third-party processing and retention, is being independently reviewed. The application must not request broader access than needed for its disclosed functions.

See the privacy information for the currently established facts and outstanding pre-production disclosures. We will not represent an unverified security control or retention promise as a verified fact.

Publisher identity

TMNM — Truth Matters News Media. This is an operator-controlled application, not a Google service. The responsible legal/trading-entity name, public support contact and final privacy/data-retention policy must be approved before external production use.
