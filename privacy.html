Public pre-production disclosure

Privacy information

For TMNM Owner Console, the operator-controlled editorial application of Truth Matters News Media.

Reviewed against original private technical-source evidence as of 22 October 2026. This notice is a pre-production disclosure, not a representation that Google's OAuth or platform publishing has been approved.

Important limitation. The operator identity, public support/contact method, complete deployed Google permission inventory, final processing recipients and retention/deletion schedules are not yet signed off. The app is not being offered for public OAuth sign-in or external user onboarding.

1. What the application does

The TMNM Owner Console is intended for private editorial administration, including reviewing operator-selected media and Google-hosted documents, preparing draft material and managing future publishing workflows that require separate operator authorisation.

2. Google data that may be accessed

Existing inspected desktop code requests permissions for Google Drive, Google Docs and certain Google Apps Script operations. With a valid grant, this may include the contents and metadata of documents selected or used by the operator, together with relevant project or deployment metadata. The final production scope inventory, granted access and deployed client configuration have not yet been verified. The inspected module does not request a YouTube scope; separate YouTube work must not be treated as covered by this client.

3. How Google data would be used

Authorised information would be used only to support the owner-selected editorial and technical workflows for which access is sought. Google account data must not be accessed for unrelated advertising or profiling purposes. This static website does not ask for your Google credentials or obtain OAuth tokens.

4. Storage, access and retention

The original application has local operating components and private evidence or audit records. Existing source investigations describe protected credential-handling mechanisms, but current account grant status and complete deployed data-storage behavior have not been independently confirmed. Editorial documents may already reside in the operator's Google Drive. No fixed deletion date is represented here as an established system guarantee. A final category-by-category retention, backup, access and deletion policy is required before wider use.

5. Processing and sharing

Workflows may involve Google Drive, Google Docs, Apps Script and, where separately authorised, other publishing or AI services. Which categories of data go to which recipient, the actual deployed integrations and their retention practices must be verified before enabling them for external users. This page makes no promise that every integration is active, or that every processor's data policies have been assessed.

6. Revoking access and requesting deletion

Google permissions, if granted, can be reviewed and revoked through your Google Account connections settings. Revoking a grant does not by itself erase past documents, local audit records, backups or third-party copies. A verified operator contact and deletion-request procedure will be supplied before external user onboarding. Do not put sensitive account or personal information in a public GitHub issue.

7. Security, rights and changes

All platform/account credentials and the protected operator system remain subject to separate owner controls; this website does not store Google account credentials. Actual security and retention statements will be updated when the deployed configuration and operating policies have been independently checked. This informational notice does not authorise publishing or data transfers.

8. Operator contact and production readiness

Organisation: Truth Matters News Media (TMNM). The responsible legal or trading operator and monitored public privacy-contact email have not yet been confirmed for display. Until that information and the final retention and processing disclosures are approved, this page should be treated as a pre-production privacy notice, not a final policy for a public-facing OAuth application.
