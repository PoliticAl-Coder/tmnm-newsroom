TMNM Meta Publisher

TMNM Meta Publisher is an editorial workflow application operated under Truth Matters News Media. It assists its authorised operator in organising and editing Google-hosted documents, reviewing article materials and preparing separately authorised publishing work.

Google OAuth status: The existing External app is marked In production, but its branding and sensitive or restricted Google permissions remain under verification. This informational website does not itself sign users in, collect Google passwords or activate publication. Platform account connections and publishing require separate authorisation.
Privacy policyTerms of use

How the app uses Google

Google Drive

With a valid account grant, the desktop client may read and manage Drive file contents and metadata in support of editorial workflows. The requested Drive permission can permit broad access, not only a single folder.

Google Docs

The app may read and edit document content and coordinate drafts or reviews through authorised Google Docs operations.

Google Apps Script

The inspected client includes project and deployment read operations and an external-request permission for Apps Script. Current deployed scripts and the full effective permission set remain under separate technical review.

Operator-controlled publishing

The application also supports preparation and review of editorial materials for future publishing workflows. This website is not itself a social-platform connection, YouTube uploader or publication authorisation. Google data is only to be used for the functions disclosed to the account holder and subject to applicable Google Limited Use requirements.

Privacy, data and contact

Google information accessed under an authorisation can be used in Drive/Docs and may produce local workflow or audit records. Retention, transfer and deletion details, including known implementation limitations, are described in our privacy policy. For consent, app support or privacy enquiries, email awhistler@gmail.com.

TMNM is the owner-operated editorial identity. This application is not developed, approved or verified by Google. The final operator legal/trading designation and full deployed-scope audit are still being confirmed before broader user access.
